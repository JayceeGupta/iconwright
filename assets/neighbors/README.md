# Neighbor icons

Stand-in apps drawn next to your icon in the scene mockups, so you can judge it among familiar logos. Downloaded once from [Iconify](https://iconify.design):

- `logos:*` from [SVG Logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos) (CC0 1.0). The logos are trademarks of their owners and are used here only to illustrate a desktop.
- `fluent-emoji-flat:*` from [Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji) (MIT).
- `steam.svg` is `thesvg-color:steam` from [theSVG](https://github.com/glincker/thesvg) (MIT): the full-color Steam icon, a blue gradient circle with a white mark. `steam-glyph.svg` is the single-color `logos:steam`, used where a recolor needs the bare logo.
- `simple-icons:*` from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0 1.0).

On Windows, the MCP `preview_icon` tool uses your own pinned apps instead.
