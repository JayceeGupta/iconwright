What you can ask for

Everything on your taskbar and desktop

    Put my games in the right column.

    Put it all back how it was.

Set up

  1. Copy the promptIt tells your agent how to install iconwright and where to learn it.
  2. Paste it into your agentClaude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any agent with a terminal.
  3. Ask for a changeTry “Make my taskbar icons white”, then “undo”.

Safety

Safe to try

iconwright changes shortcuts and one registry value, the same things you could change by hand. Safety and risks

  • Undo for everythingOriginal icons and pinned apps are saved before they change.
  • One restart per changePin changes are batched into one Explorer restart, with a dry run to check first.
  • Rolls back on failureIf a pin change fails halfway, everything goes back exactly as it was.
  • Stays on your PCThe only network calls are icon searches and downloads from Iconify.

Make your taskbar yours

Taskbar and desktop changes run on Windows 10 and 11. Designing and exporting icons also runs on macOS and Linux.
