Safe to try

iconwright changes shortcuts and one registry value, the same things you could change by hand. Safety and risks

  • Undo for everythingOriginal icons and pinned apps are saved before they change.
  • One restart per changePin changes are batched into one Explorer restart, with a dry run to check first.
  • Rolls back on failureIf a pin change fails halfway, everything goes back exactly as it was.
  • Stays on your PCThe only network calls are icon searches and downloads from Iconify.
