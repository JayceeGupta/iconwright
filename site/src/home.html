From an idea to an icon in four steps

Each step is one small image, so iterating costs your agent almost nothing and costs you a glance. Nothing is saved until you pick.

  1. Find artwork

    Search more than 200,000 open-source icons and brand logos. Results come back as one numbered sheet: say “number 7” instead of guessing from names.

    Tool: search_icons

  2. Compare looks

    Rows are artwork, columns are styles: colors, backgrounds, gradients, shapes. Each cell shows the icon large and at 24 px on dark and light. Pick a cell like “B3”.

    Tool: design_icons

  3. See it in place

    Mock the icon up on a Windows taskbar, Start menu, desktop, File Explorer, a browser tab, the macOS Dock, an iPhone and Android. On Windows, it sits between your own pinned apps.

    Tool: preview_icon

  4. Ship it

    Get a Windows .ico, a macOS .icns, a complete favicon and web-app set, or PNGs at every size. On Windows, put it on any app's Start menu and taskbar shortcuts in the same breath.

    Tools: make_icon, app_apply_icon

Your taskbar, restyled in one sentence

iconwright knows how Windows keeps pinned apps, so your agent can restyle every icon, pin, unpin and reorder apps, and tidy the desktop. Changes that need Explorer to restart are batched into one restart.

Read the taskbar and apps guide or the desktop guide.

Make all my taskbar icons white.

taskbar_status()

app_apply_icon(apps: [13 pinned apps], source: "app", style: "color=white")

Done: 13 icons are now white, each from the app's own artwork. Want them back? Say “undo” and I'll restore the originals.

Safe to try

iconwright changes shortcuts and one registry value, the same things you'd change by hand. Everything it changes, it can put back.

Read about safety and the risks

  • Undo for everythingOriginal icons are recorded before the first change; the pinned-apps list is backed up before every change.
  • One Explorer restart per changePin, unpin and reorder in a single call, with a dry run to check first.
  • Rolls back on failureIf a pin change fails halfway, the registry and shortcuts go back exactly as they were.
  • Stays on your PCThe only network calls are icon searches and downloads from Iconify.
